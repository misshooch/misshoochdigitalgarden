Masturbation, I have had a particularly long romance with. Probably my most enduring and faithful relationship! When I first moved to Australia I was masturbating for perhaps four hours a day. It was quite problematic. Sometimes I would be out and have to come home to masturbate. I do it routinely. I do not need any stimulus at all, and I don't really need to think of anything. There is something almost absurd about this degree of self-sufficiency. I can be entirely alone and yet so completely occupied by pleasure.

I wonder if I'm just especially sensitive to sensation, or if I've built up an unusually intimate relationship with my own body. Sometimes I don't think there's much difference between intellectual and physical pleasure for me. A thought can get me going as easily as a touch, a sentence as easily as a sound. Maybe the body and the mind aren't as separate as we like to think.

I'm also curious about where pleasure stops and compulsion starts. When does chasing pleasure stop being freedom and start being a kind of captivity? Sometimes it feels like my body makes demands my mind hasn't agreed to. But I don't want to see it as shameful either. It's a bit silly to be embarrassed by the thing that gives me so much pleasure! Although, a while ago it got so compulsive that I was injuring myself and couldn't stop. That did feel problematic, another bit of my greed and excess.

Maybe I'm just very easily moved. By music, by words, by ideas, by my own imagination. It is quite ridiculous that I can think myself into an orgasm, but there we are! What I want to know is whether this is too much sexuality or too much sensitivity. And whether there's really any difference.

I started at age 7 and in the time between then and now found myself to get very quick with it. I suppose, I will often use it as a means of emotional regulation. It does warrant some caution though, I do need to be careful not to condition myself to certain, things, thoughts or states of mind.
