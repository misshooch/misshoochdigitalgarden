>it seems I cannot uphold a stable version of myself without borrowing bits from those on the outside. Day 3 in my room. I quickly lose all the pillars of the self. To conclude, I need a 'you' for there to be a 'me' and without such interactions, or intangible ones the 'me' tries to fulfil all possibilities, thus inhabiting none. I cannot get far enough away fro myself and suddenly everything I do or say is compromising. 
![5CE20681-06AA-4E8A-B413-F5000EC090EB_1_105_c.jpeg](/img/user/5CE20681-06AA-4E8A-B413-F5000EC090EB_1_105_c.jpeg)Raisa, sweet sweet Raisa, wrote a wonderful [substack](https://lunaisaaa.substack.com/p/10-months-sober?r=2h7btz&utm_campaign=post-expanded-share&utm_medium=post%20viewer) yesterday. It moved me to tears. It is funny, she comes across as though she has no internal wobbles or struggles. I cannot lie - I have ALWAYS - wanted to be mentioned in someone's work. It would be confirmation that I exist and that I am noteworthy, that I matter, that I have gravity. I am **Max(ienne)** in her work - which i found so beautiful. It is not narcissistic to wish to see how others see you and i can see she sees me with love and affection and i am so moved. 

> I was once dating a musician very innocently - bless him - I think i ate him alive. he wrote a song about me 'Bold (may have been an 'a' as i had a buzzcut at this time) girl' - he performed it to me at a show, in the crowd. A girl that liked him stood by the way side and cried. My friend I took was in disbelief, mouth agog, watching a very dramatic, romantic and dreamy declaration. It was a beautiful experience but also I knew in that moment that I must stop emotionally and sexually edging the poor boy. He was a delight, he was too young for me, I am so grateful he gave me that experience nonetheless. I wish him all the best and I wish I had not interfered in his life.
