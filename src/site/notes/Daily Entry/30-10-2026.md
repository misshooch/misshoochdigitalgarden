Today, i called off work as a use of tit-for-tat in game theory. I hope it works. If it does not work, I do not think i care too much. In the gig economy there are plenty of jobs. Anyway, i am pretty resilient. I have been extremely poor in my time. poor and hungry. I don't think it will get to that again and if it does then i shall be poor and hungry until i am not. But i am starting to actualise a little bit now - the EMDR is implanting some positive thoughts in my head. 'i am powerful,' 'my body belongs to me,' 'i deserve nice things,' when i was being reprogrammed i remarked 'this is what it used to feel like when i sniffed ketamine!' both me and my therapist felt the whole thing was rather unintentionally on the nose... But now i am realising i am powerful! insofar as i have agency! I have power over my life. i Think i was living in bad faith before, hoping that if i did enough of things i didn't like that in the end i would be living a life i did like! well, that is just absurd. I am so pleased i realise this now. More likely, i must live a life i like, in order to live a life i like. I think i have resigned myself to the fact i will likely not have a career of prestige and i will not be Joan of Arc or any other martyr who invariably is only revered posthumously and who, ultimately, must die. no, i shall live and i shall live well. Because for me *living* is largely a state of mind. Now I am using my free will again and demolishing self-imposed limiting beliefs i am so excited to live for the rest of my life! i hope it is long! anyway, i won't be a martyr at work. i will leave. life is far too precious for an emotionally incontinent boss to be verbally dousing me in a diarrhoea of vitriol.
