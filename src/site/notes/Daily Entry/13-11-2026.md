Dear Diary,

### Whoever said "be yourself" had clearly never met me.

That's what I was thinking ALL night. I had a nice day. I showed Ben there was more to life than mortgage brokering. He came over and we went for a walk. He was on the clock, but Outlook was down, so I just mixed some music for him as he took calls, and then sat him on the grass and showed him how to make chainmail. I felt wonderful, snivelling but good. Then I left the house for work, and some protective mental barrier that I must usually have, and be unaware of, left me. HOLY SHIT. I became a 2/10. I could feel the tears in my ducts, but I would not release them. I felt hateful. Mostly towards myself.

> I have been trying to be myself, but I've concluded that that is awful advice for someone like me, and actually, the objective should be to try to be less like myself.

> I felt so, so, so humiliated. For hours, I felt humiliated. WHAT AM I DOING?!?! WHY AM I PUTTING THIS ONLINE?!?!?! I cannot keep up the performance of being like everyone else. 

	It struck me... maybe this is real vulnerability. I think I often do the vulnerable thing, but honestly, it mimics vulnerability without actually being vulnerable. But now I am more exposed than ever, and I am vulnerable. I am hoping to be greeted with compassion, but I don't think, at this point in my life, I can be so open to others and be sincere when I say that I don't give a fuck how I am received. I do give a fuck at the moment. I do not have enough self-assurance to contend otherwise.


> It also struck me that... I think this whole world is performance art. People are playing parts, and they are actors, and they expect the other actors to keep to the script. I HATE THE SCRIPT. I think it is bonkers. I think it is all ABSURD. Why do we need different versions of ourselves? It is like _Severance_. I had this thought more hashed out on the walk home.

> ==In a flow state, then, I was... listening to Radiohead's 'Where I End and You Begin', which is today's SOTD (song of the day)==. Rain was hitting my face. I love to feel each drop. I basically love everything that makes me feel. To me, that song is extremely romantic and erotic, and I'm sure other people feel the same way, but I am equally sure that lots of people won't. AND THAT IS OKAY. IT DOES NOT MEAN ANYTHING IS BETTER OR WORSE BUT I OTHER PEOPLE OUT THERE WILL AGREE AND I SHOULD FIND THEM!!!!!! AND IF PEOPLE THING THAT THE CONTENTS OF THAT SONG ARE WEIRD THEN IT IS OKAY IF THEY GET LOST. take a hike. 

> What's the trade? I forfeit myself for the palatability of others to obtain security? That's what the trade-off always feels like.

> "Just be yourself" is good advice for everyone who is not me. Because everything I do is embarrassing. Because I am, uniquely, a hopeless, tragic and humiliating case.

## Then I realised... I am FUCKING SICK of thinking about myself like that.

> I must exercise my thoughts down different neural pathways.  
> This incessant self-castigation MUST STOP.

> My world is literally just what I think it to be.  
> Maybe being me is the worst fate to befall anyone? Then what?  
> It is FUNNY. It is all funny. I am embarrassed by my own innocence and fundamental discordance with how the world actually works and how people actually operate. I will look back and laugh about the silly things I did... like putting this website on a CV.

> That was NOT me playing the game of the job market very well. But if I am me, and I continue to be me, then I imagine I will weed out all the people, places and things that do not fit me.

> My life will not end. Nothing will stop. I will carry on. I was born dead, and I always thought, because I kept trying to kill myself, that I wanted to die. But I think I have an incredibly fierce will to live!

> I WILL keep living. I DO NOT GIVE A FUCK what this bizarro, prescriptive world thinks I ought to do. I think I have more sense than that. Why should I care about the opinions of people who cannot even truly look themselves in the eyes??

> This diary is written by someone who is in a VERY difficult and strained period of their life, and I ask you to bear that in mind always <3

> I think I will take the analytics off... It's not useful for me to see all the views and the countries and the IP addresses. Love you 4 Tyn... haha JK I WOULD NEVER DOX ANYONE.

> Not useful to me. Right now, I need to remember I am literally 7 years old again, every day, and somehow alone and supposed to be holding down a job and a life when, actually, things have been very hard, and it's kind of all there is. From the moment I open my eyes to when I close them for sleep, I am having flashbacks. Life is hard right now! It is not the best version of me! THAT'S OKAY! I just need to stay sober and stay safe <3 <3 and stop fucking picking on myself because I am not this unattainable version of a human being that is more like an angel than a human being! I am fallible. I try and fail and then try again.

Goodnight, Miss HOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOCH.
