### the private vs public.

I was going to post something onto my private instagram story and then thought i'd post it on my main - but in the end i wasn't happy with my work. But, it got me thinking about the private vs public.

I have a lot of people on my private story and back in teh day, it had a name for itself. People would request to go on it, it was just so funny. As my life is bereft of boundless humour at present the private story isn't that much of a treat.

But, it goes to show that despite my willingness to be public - _access all areas_ - there are still some areas I reserve for a select few. And, as I still use my personal journal, there are still some areas reserved for just myself.

> My personal journal is the toilet cubicle; the place i go where i can even escape myself.

## I used to be like that.

One girl checks her phone frequently, the lack of text message from her boyfriend puts her in a bad mood. It is a fine line to walk.

I used to be like that because unfortunaetley, in my previous relationship. i felt so insecure. It started on rocky ground. He slept with someone else when we were 'exclusive' - to me now this all feels arbitrary but to me then, i felt it proof that i could not be loved.

That was the best i deserved and so, rather than do the thing every cell in my body was telling me to do and leave, i stayed.

I cried in his bed and told him to fuck me.

And so began our relationship built on insecurity, lack of trust and self-abandonment.

**I self abandonded relentlessly.**

I used to always want to cohabit with someone and have a bedroom each and a conjugal bedroom. We lived in the same room on (not that straight forward) for 3 years. And, over those three years I would check my phone and not understand how he could be ignoring me so much.

He said he loved me and i never believed it. I was unable to accept it.

> It is interesting to me now, that there are people who have never once told me they love me but I know and trust that they do.

At work, one of my colleguses always helps me with the heavy lifting. I do not ask and he does so without wanting much thanks.

This moves me.

This shows me he cares about me, he does not say it and he does not ask for anything in return.

I feel more love in that action, in so many small actions by so many different people than I ever did in that relationship.

## Related and unrelated, I have been thinking a lot about eroticism.

_Which grows and blooms in separation._

We are so bad at being separate.

In this moment I am separated from Someone. But I find there is a lot being communicated in our lack of communication. At least for me, it says

> 'I respect you.'
> 
> 'I trust you.'
> 
> 'I am secure.'

I feel delighted that this person has their own life separate from me.

**A whole universe that does not belong to me but, that I am invited in to.**

I find myself wondering about them. Talking to them in my head. I will forget all the things I want to share with them. But, i think this is really as it ought to be.

### We suck at being separate. Thus, we suck at being ourselves.

We are, in our earthly bodies, inextricably separate.

Born along to die alone.

And, i think it is in recognising this separation that one truly learns what it means to love. To have 0 expectations of another. Just an appreciation of what is shared and what is together.

The reason so many of us are unable to self-love is because we are so exposed to ourselves, we see ourselves, shit and masticate food and have bad thoughts. We know ourselves so much it becomes easy to hate ourselves.

With a bit of separation, when someone can maintain an unknowable quality there is always something to love and admire.

## To decide is to cut off,

I have been thinking too, about the etymology of 'decide.'

to decide is to choose something and _not_ choose everything else.

> There is something inside me, that knows a great deal more than I do, that has decided.

I have decided something in particular and also a great many other things. I have chosen and not chosen everything else. Probably against my 'better judgement' but it is so.

I feel totally at ease with these decisions and they will reveal themselves to me in due course.

### This is another failing in modernity that I see: we have decision fatigue.

We cannot choose anyone or anything - because we are so sure there may be better. But, it is such a passive way to live. To live at the whim of 'feelings' something, we are not, something we simply have - momentarily.

**In our indecision so much passes us by.**

We are afraid to commit. I was afraid to commit to Max(ienne) I am afraid to commit to an identity.

Just as Plath's Fig Tree analogy - to choose one is to not choose the rest but by choosing none I watch the things before me rot.

Whilst we, i think, should commit. We should also know we can change our minds.

I think I will refrain from promising an eternity to myself or anyone else. But, know that I can promise all that I have, which is _now._

And, can promise to keep the stakeholders informed when and if the now expires.

I can also promise with assurance to try.

**I think everything worth having, doing or being takes time, patience and work.**

## To me 'i love you' means 'I accept you'.

This is another thing that I have been mismatched with in my previous endeavours. To me, 'i love you' means not necessarily that I feel overwhelmingly in love with you or even that I like you, in that given moment in time.

But that I will choose to love you (Bell Hooks).

I will choose to love you even when I don't feel like it. I will choose to act in good faith, I will choose to have patience. I will choose to do right by you, be a kind and trustworthy person and to accept you.

> I see you and I will not look away. I will not turn my cheek, nor my head.

I love you means I will do what I think is right and right by you even if that means jeopardising my access to you.

### To love someone, I think is a greater privilege than being loved.

It does more for you than it does for them. To love a person or a thing. Truly. Means I don't care if you hate me, I will carry on.

And, so much personal insight can be gleaned from that. I think to love others is a great act of self-love, self-determination and self-growth.

**Even, selfless love is, to me, selfish.**
