Dear Diary:
## covid.
>I probably had it yday which is why i cried so much. I found i was confronted by a lot to do with Autism. The day arrived demanding to be mourned. I found my copy of An Apprenticehip or The Book of Pleasures which i began reading so so so long ago. I annotate and underline everything. I began to reread it and it is almost mystically timed. Excited! I am pleased to have time to rest that i do not feel guilty about. I am having many mystical experiences at the moment. I am living acutely. 

> [!NOTE] 
> My life has become charged with a certain zeal, a presence that is almost, but not quite, tangible.


#### i feel as though i am about to enjoy things again. The destiny quest of australia continues. It is interesting how for years I have known what would happen to me at the age of 26 and 29. It is exciting to know this means, as things are happening at 26, that I will likely live to 29 - at least!

The next phase of my journey is to unmask. Which i feel extremely reserved about. It no longer serves me and i have been masking much more since being in this country. 

>I think, I am being unlocked and accepted. I am feeling so accepted - in a way that is so pronounced. It is so awe-inspiring I do not even want to name it because to name it, would narrow it. 

> i think the covid and the emdr and the burnout are showing me how all these spinning plates actually would be better smashed. 


>I am laying down but i am tachycardic. I must be careful - I cannot cause any more harm or damage to my heart or nervous system. 

>Usually, i feel very depressed when i am sick, but today, I feel very content and without fear.

>I have brain fog which is less pronounced now I am off the Wellbutrin, it may be from sobriety but I think it is from the Isotretonoin which means i forget words and grammar rules. Maybe i will just have to relearn everything. Thinking used to be my flow state. 

##### i used to feel as though I knew everything but had forgotten so when i was learning, I was just relearning. I have impressed myself at times with some of the things i have inexplicably known but the more aware I become of these things, the less able i am to tap into these things.

> i remember dating someone once, who had not finished school, it was a source of shame for him but i thought it was cool. I don't mean it is cool to not finish school but i think it is cool that at that time, he was so assured he did not wish to follow the convention - that he did not. I love seeing people who do the unusual thing - for whatever reason. And even if the reason for these things are not 'good' i think it is wonderful to see passion in someone, even if the person is passionate about self-sabotage. Passion can be transferred - dispassion harder to mutate. 


on my flyer i said i like anamnesis and currently I am experiencing it in nearly all forms!!!!! What an untold joy!!!!!!!!!!!

>I have a talent for identifying slot machines.
