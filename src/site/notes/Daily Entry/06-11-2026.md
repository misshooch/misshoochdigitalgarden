Dear diary,



>[!note] these thoughts have been moving me a lot. this feels vulnerable.  

>**I just walked home from work. In the torrential rain, sheets of rain. Listening to ==Girls by Death in Vegas== on repeat. I was beaming and laughing. I love the rain! I love having waterproof skin. Sometimes I think I might be the happiest person in the world. I knew I was certainly the happiest person on the street. I feel guided from my chest with this incredible magnetic light and love. This feeling I so often get to feel is, I sincerely imagine, the closest a person can feel to the divine. It is a force so powerful I am compelled to believe in something. A belief that good things are to come?! A belief of hope. I am so glad to be returning. Returning to a person who can love boldly, and bravely and unconditionally. The journey I am on, back home, back to myself is the best journey I have ever known. And how lucky I am to be alive and get to venture down this path. How lucky I am to live and get to know myself and learn who I am. How lucky I am to feel so much gladness, to be glad and know the other side of the joy and the gladness and only know that that allows me to feel it even more. I would not change a single bad thing that has happened to me because if it hadn’t I wouldn’t be me and I don’t think it would be possible to be so happy because I wouldn’t know how possible is to feel so sad. i feel high yet i am sober** 

  

>**I smile at the world and the world so often smiles back at me. I am in love with it and, it with me. People can be so kind and thoughtful and I want to drink all of what is meant by the human spirit. People make my life into something entirely special, their good humour and idiosyncrasies and beliefs. I love how human everyone is. I love how animal they are without knowing they are animals. I love remembering I am an animal myself. For a long time I forgot this and, I thought I had to be strict to be good -- but I don’t -- I can be an animal. I can do whatever I like, I am just an animal. A happy animal. A lucky animal. A grateful animal.**

  

>**But an animal that takes big swings. All this joy is how I have been feeling - today. And I may feel entirely differently tomorrow but it doesn’t change the sincerity with which I feel it today. posting these writings, with increased candour, I am starting to critique myself through the imaginary lens of others and I wonder if others may pathologise me with something. in my arrogance, i think i can preempt how others will respond to me and i ironically use this as a tool to judge and shame myself. 
>
>I have spent my whole life objecting to my nature; forcing myself to do ‘normal’ things; 'normal' bedtimes, 'normal' habits, 'normal' thoughts... I am peeling this back and it is revealing to me an identity I am enamoured with but, one people may regard as unwell. Or, one i am *worried* people may regard as unwell. I have been afraid of these parts, I have been afraid and I have shamed them. I forgot that there is no shame. That shame is not natural, it is social. There is no aspect of myself I should want to distance myself from! I should want to embrace all aspects, accept them and tell them I love them. If they are bad ever, which they are not, I should want to find out why not try and hide them. I shouldn’t tell these parts that I wish I didn’t have them because of how I may have imagined someone would respond to them. How unkind!**

  

>**I am really learning to love myself. I find it easier. Non-linear but easier. I charm myself but I worry I may be too unwell to love. That is why i worry about being perceived as unwell. It is a womanly thing. I imagine men do not lament being viewed in this way. I know people have always fallen in love with me, but it is an unsustainable love. I tire them. I do not mean to. I think I might be emotionally laborious. I am not often ill-tempered but I get so sad. And, I think that can be hard. I don’t think people can tolerate the intensity and immensity of my emotions. Even the good ones. I think when people have a greater awareness of me, even the awareness drains them. To look at me and know - exhausts people. I expect them to love me unconditionally but they cannot. It is with condition. The condition often feels like they can know the things but I can never show the things. I must be well. Even though, we all know, I can never be well. I must be like everyone else even though I had never even really met an everyone else. I didn’t even know how much my yard stick differed. i hide myself so much from people. My friend Ocea always used to turn up and see me, particularly when i said i didn't want to because i was below a 7/10 for fun. She was trying to teach me that i still deserve love. Just for being. even when i can't bring anything. Its funny because i can't wait to love someone on the days when they cannot get away from themselves. I would love to love someone who is sad and in bed and i make them get out of bed and drive them somewhere and we both do it in our pyjamas and have no plan but some fresh air and then i made them a hot drink and some food and we sit wrapped up and watch a film and they were sad the whole time but i loved them enough. But the thought of someone doing that for me makes my skin crawl! Ocea has taught me a lot about love. thank you Ocea. 

  
>**Maybe this is why I have been so committed to being aloof. Not something I consider myself to be, as I am plagued with an almost constant self-awareness, but I suppose I am. And, I have no plan to change that.**

  
>**I know my previous relationships have all ended because I am too much. They can’t handle ‘it’. Whatever ineffable thing ‘it’ is. It often leaves me without closure. No one can tell me why they want to end it. They always seem to be in love with me too. But they cannot be with me. I don’t mind being without the love and just being the one who gives it. I am happy to give the love and I know that I am not owed and I do not own. I want the people I love to be happy. But, I think I worry that one day I will want the love. I think I am so pleased I have been so alone. To love is my salience not to be loved. But still, I think I might want it soon, or now. How soon is now?**

  

>**I saw a young couple today leaving my gym - they had a baby and they were playing with it like a toy. They were so cool and their baby looked very happy. I worry that I will not resolve everything in time. That someone will know that I actually have no idea on so many important things.**

  

>**At work, a table asked me how old the children should be before they’re allowed to have a same bed sleepover with their partner. I thought it’s not really the parents business to be policing stuff like that. Everyone else said 18. I was horrified with myself. How could I not know?! How could I not know that parents are supposed to protect their children from that. Then I wonder all the other things I do not know. There are so many things that I am learning now. I do not want to pass anything on. I am so pleased I do not have a child, yet. I have been aching to meet my child for a while. I write them letters sometimes. But I am not ready. Sometimes I think the only reason I want to have a child is so I can have something to appropriatley love with the intensity that I love all things.**

  

>**I am trying though. I am trying harder than I have ever tried. And, I know things are changing and maybe one day, with some luck I can have someone to stroke my head. Someone who will play me some music just for me, read to me whilst i am in the bath and laugh with.** 

>**i like to imagine i am looking out the window of a train with headphones on and someone who loves me picks all the songs for my daydreams.**

  
**For now though, I will just try and keep happy and remain grateful for this period. Every period of my life is always the best one yet.**

Goodbye, Miss Hooch
