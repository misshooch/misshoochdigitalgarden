We eventually tired of this and got uncomfortable and left. I said i wanted to snitch but then i thought not to. I had a bad experience with 'snitching' in the past. In year 11 i, along with about 10 other people, got expelled. It started with my friend and me getting caught with a ==Q of weed in her locker before a sleepover.== She got pulled into thje headteachers office and called me off speaker phone saying that the headteacher wanted to speak to me. I said 'fuck, is it about all the shoplifting?' This was a faux pas on my part - i admit that. As, it hadn't been about that but it would be afterwards. Anyway, i got interviewed after school for about 3 hours - police threatened to be called etc. This carried on the next day at school when it was revealed to me that the school knew the location in which we smoked at lunchtimes - implicating a lot more people. I tried to do the right thing and spread this message to my commrades so they could try and get their stories straight. Low and behold the school did speak to them all but rather than being viewed as a friend, I was turned into a foe. EVERYONE accused me of being the snitch. I was spat at and pushed as i walked around school and the local gang (who are now all in prison) had publicly started to put hits on me! My firstborn is dead apparently. Anyway, this experience has given me a bit of a complex about 'snitching'.
